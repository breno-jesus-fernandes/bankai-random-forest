# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Bankai contributors and maintainers are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or nationality.

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project events. It also applies in public spaces when someone is representing the project or its community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas and work, rather than attacking people.
- Considering how wording may be understood by others.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or sexualized language or imagery.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Other conduct that a reasonable person would consider unprofessional or harmful in a project community.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code. They may reject contributions or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Maintainers are expected to apply these measures fairly and consistently.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [breno.de.jesus-fernandes@gmail.com](mailto:breno.de.jesus-fernandes@gmail.com). Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports promptly, keep the reporter's identity confidential to the extent reasonably possible, and take action appropriate to the circumstances.
